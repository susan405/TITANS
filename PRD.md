Vrya --- Product Requirements Document

Version: 1.0
Document status: Project proposal
Date: 22 November 2026



1. Product overview

Product name: Vrya
Product category: Skill marketplace and peer-to-peer learning
platform

Vrya is a platform that connects people who want to learn practical
skills with people who are able to teach them. Instructors can create
profiles, list the skills they offer, set their own prices and earn
money when learners book their lessons.

The idea is straightforward: people should be able to earn from
knowledge they already have, while others should be able to find useful
lessons without having to rely only on expensive training programmes.

Product statement:Learn a skill. Teach a skill. Earn from your
knowledge.

2. The problem

People have skills that could be useful to others, but they do not
always have a simple way to find learners and earn from teaching. At the
same time, people who want to learn practical skills may find it
difficult to locate an instructor they can trust or afford.

The main problems Vrya aims to address are:

-   Cost of training:Some courses and private lessons are too
    expensive for learners.
-   Finding the right instructor:Learners may not know where to
    find someone who can teach a particular skill.
-   Unused knowledge:Students, freelancers and professionals may
    have useful skills but limited ways to turn that knowledge into
    income.
-   Scheduling difficulties:Some training options do not offer the
    flexibility learners need.
-   Trust:It can be difficult to judge an instructor's experience
    before paying for a lesson.

Vrya addresses the gap between people who have knowledge to share and
people who want to learn.

3. Proposed solution

Vrya will operate as a marketplace for paid lessons.

Instructors will be able to:

-   Create a profile and describe their experience.
-   List the skills and subjects they can teach.
-   Set prices for individual lessons.
-   Add their available days and times.
-   Receive and manage lesson bookings.
-   Track completed lessons and earnings.

Learners will be able to:

-   Search for skills and instructors.
-   Compare prices, profiles and reviews.
-   Choose a lesson and available time.
-   Pay through the platform.
-   Attend the lesson and leave a review.
-   Keep track of their learning activity.

After a lesson is completed, the instructor receives the amount due to
them, less Vrya's disclosed platform commission.

4. Target users

Vrya will initially focus on university students, young professionals
and freelancers in Nigeria. The platform can expand to other groups as
it grows.

Instructors

Potential instructors include:

-   Students with skills they can teach.
-   Freelancers and independent professionals.
-   Software developers and designers.
-   Digital marketers and content creators.
-   People with practical skills such as photography, baking, fashion
    design and tailoring.
-   Professionals with relevant work experience.

Learners

Potential learners include:

-   Students who want to develop additional skills.
-   Beginners who want to learn a new subject.
-   Job seekers improving their employability.
-   Entrepreneurs learning business-related skills.
-   People interested in personal or professional development.

A user should be able to use Vrya as both a learner and an instructor
without creating two separate accounts.

5. Product goals

The first version of Vrya should:

1.  Give instructors a straightforward way to offer paid lessons.
2.  Help learners find instructors that match their needs and budget.
3.  Support booking and payment through the platform.
4.  Track lesson completion and instructor earnings.
5.  Give learners a way to review their experience.
6.  Build enough trust for people to feel comfortable using the
    marketplace.

The main thing to validate is whether learners are willing to pay for
lessons and whether instructors are willing to offer their skills
through the platform.

6. Main features

6.1 Account creation and login

Users should be able to register, log in, log out and recover their
passwords. During setup, they can choose to teach, learn or do both.

The platform should support profile editing and protect account
information.

6.2 Instructor profiles

Each instructor profile should contain:

-   Name and profile picture.
-   Short biography.
-   Skills offered.
-   Relevant experience.
-   Teaching description.
-   Portfolio or examples of work, where available.
-   Lesson prices.
-   Availability.
-   Ratings and reviews.

Instructors should be able to list more than one skill and create
separate lesson offerings.

6.3 Skill listings and discovery

Learners should be able to search for a skill and view instructors who
offer it.

Useful filters include:

-   Skill category.
-   Price range.
-   Instructor rating.
-   Experience level.
-   Available dates and times.
-   Beginner, intermediate or advanced lessons.
-   Online or approved in-person lessons.

Possible categories include technology, business, creative skills,
practical skills and academic subjects.

6.4 Booking and scheduling

A learner should be able to open an instructor's listing, choose a
lesson, select an available time and confirm the booking.

The booking flow should show the lesson details and full price before
payment. Instructors should be able to manage their availability and
booking requests.

6.5 Payments and instructor earnings

Payment is central to Vrya's business model.

Learners pay for lessons through an integrated payment provider. Vrya
records the payment and the booking. Once the lesson has been completed
according to the platform's rules, the instructor's eligible earnings
can be paid out.

Vrya earns a commission from completed paid lessons. The commission rate
must be shown clearly before a learner confirms a booking.

The platform should provide:

-   Payment history for learners.
-   Earnings history for instructors.
-   Payout status and transaction records.
-   Cancellation and refund handling.
-   A process for reporting and resolving payment disputes.

Payment processing should be handled by a suitable provider. Vrya should
not store users' raw card details. The team must check the provider's
current requirements, fees and payout support before choosing one. Any
arrangement that holds funds before payout must be supported by the
provider and reviewed against applicable regulations.

6.6 Reviews and ratings

After a completed lesson, the learner should be able to rate the
instructor and leave feedback.

Only learners with a completed booking should be allowed to review that
lesson. Users should also be able to report inappropriate behaviour or
misleading listings.

6.7 Learner dashboard

The learner dashboard should show:

-   Upcoming lessons.
-   Completed lessons.
-   Learning history.
-   Saved instructors.
-   Spending history.
-   Reviews submitted.

6.8 Instructor dashboard

The instructor dashboard should show:

-   Active skill and lesson listings.
-   Upcoming bookings.
-   Completed sessions.
-   Earnings and payout status.
-   Ratings and reviews.

6.9 Messaging and lesson delivery

Learners and instructors need a way to communicate about bookings. The
platform should provide booking-related messages, reminders,
notifications and session details.

For the first version, online lessons can use an approved external
video-meeting link. Building a video-calling system from scratch is not
necessary for the initial release.

6.10 Admin dashboard

Administrators should be able to:

-   View and manage user accounts.
-   Review instructor profiles and verification requests.
-   Monitor bookings and transactions.
-   Handle reports and disputes.
-   Manage refunds where appropriate.
-   Remove fraudulent or misleading listings.
-   View basic platform activity.

7. Monetization model

Vrya's main source of revenue will be a commission on paid lessons.

When a learner books a lesson, the learner pays the listed price. Vrya
deducts its agreed commission, and the instructor receives the remaining
amount after the lesson has been completed and the payout conditions
have been met.

The commission percentage has not yet been finalized. It should be
decided after researching payment-provider fees, operating costs and
what instructors consider fair.

Other possible revenue sources can be considered later:

-   **Premium instructor tools:** Optional paid features for instructors
    who want additional profile or business tools.
-   **Featured listings:** Paid promotion for instructors, clearly
    labelled as sponsored.
-   **Group lessons:** Vrya may earn a commission from paid group
    sessions.
-   **Corporate training:** Organizations may use Vrya to arrange
    training for their teams.

These are future possibilities. The first release should focus on
getting the paid lesson process working properly.

8. Features that could set Vrya apart

8.1 Skill-based recommendations

Vrya could recommend instructors based on the learner's chosen skill,
current level, learning goal, budget, availability and instructor
feedback.

For example, a beginner looking for Python lessons should be shown
instructors who offer beginner-level Python teaching, rather than only
profiles that mention Python.

8.2 Skill Growth Passport

Each learner could have a record of their learning activity, including:

-   Skills studied.
-   Lessons completed.
-   Projects completed.
-   Learning milestones.
-   Instructor feedback.

This would be a record of learning on Vrya. It should not be presented
as a formal qualification or professional certification.

8.3 Instructor verification

Instructors could submit portfolios, certificates or practical examples
of their work. Vrya should explain what has been checked and avoid
suggesting that a profile is fully verified when only limited
information has been reviewed.

8.4 Group lessons

Instructors could offer a lesson to several learners at once. This could
lower the cost for each learner while allowing the instructor to teach
more people in one session.

 9. User journeys

Learner journey

1.  Create an account or log in.
2.  Search for a skill.
3.  Compare instructor profiles, prices and reviews.
4.  Select a lesson and available time.
5.  Review the details and pay.
6.  Attend the lesson.
7.  Confirm completion and leave a review.
8.  View the lesson in the learning history.

Instructor journey

1.  Create an account or log in.
2.  Set up an instructor profile.
3.  Add skills and lesson descriptions.
4.  Set prices and availability.
5.  Receive and manage booking requests.
6.  Conduct the lesson.
7.  View completed lessons and eligible earnings.
8.  Build a reputation through learner reviews.

11. Database design

The first database design should include these tables:

  Table                Purpose
  
  Users                Account information and user roles
  InstructorProfiles   Instructor biographies and verification status
  Skills               Available skill categories
  SkillListings        Skills offered by instructors
  Lessons              Lesson descriptions and prices
  Availability         Instructor schedules
  Bookings             Lesson reservations and booking status
  Payments             Learner payment records and provider references
  Payouts              Instructor payout records
  PlatformFees         Vrya commission records
  Reviews              Learner ratings and feedback
  Messages             Booking-related communication
  Notifications        Booking and payment alerts
  Reports              Complaints and disputes
  LearningProgress     Learner milestones and history

One user may have an instructor profile and can create multiple skill
listings. Learners can make multiple bookings. Each booking should be
linked to its payment record. Completed paid lessons can produce a
payout record and a platform-fee record.

Payment and payout records should remain separate. Each financial record
should include the relevant IDs, amount, status, payment-provider
reference and timestamps.

13. Non-functional requirements

-   Security: Passwords must be securely hashed, and restricted
    actions must require authorization.
-   Reliability: The system should prevent duplicate payment
    processing and duplicate payouts.
-   Performance: Search results and dashboards should load within a
    reasonable time.
-   Scalability: The architecture should allow the platform to
    support more users and transactions.
-   Usability:The interface should be straightforward for
    first-time users.
-   Privacy:Personal and payment information must be protected.
-   Maintainability:The code should be organized into reusable
    frontend components and clear backend modules.

14. Trust, safety and fraud prevention

Since Vrya connects people who may not know one another, trust needs to
be considered from the beginning.

The platform should include:

-   Appropriate instructor identity and profile checks.
-   Evidence of claimed skills where practical.
-   Reviews limited to completed bookings.
-   Reporting and blocking features.
-   Clear cancellation and refund policies.
-   Secure payment processing.
-   Records of important payment and booking actions.
-   A defined process for resolving disputes.
-   Payout rules that prevent instructors from being paid twice for the
    same booking.

Vrya should also have age-appropriate account rules and safeguards for
younger users. Online lessons should be the default. Any in-person
learning arrangements should follow suitable safety requirements.

Build in the MVP:

4.  Lesson pricing.
5.  Search and filtering.
6.  Booking and scheduling.

8.  Commission calculation.
9.  Instructor earnings dashboard.

**Leave for later releases:**

-   Built-in video conferencing.
-   Premium subscriptions.
-   A full group-learning marketplace.
-   Skill Growth Passport.
-   Advanced analytics.

16. Example of how Vrya solves the problem

Imagine a student who understands Python but does not have a
straightforward way to find people willing to pay for lessons. Another
student wants to learn Python but cannot afford an expensive training
programme.

With Vrya:

1.  The first student creates an instructor profile.
2.  They list beginner Python lessons and set a price.
3.  The second student searches for Python lessons.
4.  They compare instructor profiles and choose a lesson.
5.  They book and pay through Vrya.
6.  The instructor conducts the lesson.
7.  The booking is marked as completed.
8.  The instructor receives the amount due to them, less Vrya's
    commission.
9.  The learner can leave a review and track the lesson in their
    learning history.

The instructor earns from a skill they already have, the learner gets
access to practical teaching, and Vrya earns by providing the
marketplace and transaction process.

20. Competitive positioning

Vrya operates in the wider online learning and tutoring marketplace
space. Its intended focus is on practical skills offered by independent
instructors, with instructor-set prices, flexible booking, platform
payments and a record of learning activity.

Before making claims that Vrya is the first or only platform of its
kind, the team should research existing services and document what makes
Vrya different.

21. Product vision

Vrya --- Your knowledge has value.

Vrya aims to make it easier for people to share what they know, earn
from their skills and learn from others.

-   For learners:Find the knowledge and guidance you need.
-   For instructors: Turn your skills into an income opportunity.
-   For the platform: Make practical learning easier to access while
    supporting people who teach.

22. Recommendation and next steps

Vrya should be developed first as a paid peer-to-peer skill marketplace.
The instructor provides a lesson, the learner pays for it, and Vrya
manages discovery, booking, payment records and the platform commission.

Before development goes too far, the team should speak with potential
learners and instructors and find out:

1.  Would learners pay an independent instructor to teach a practical
    skill?
2.  Would skilled people be interested in earning money by teaching?
3.  What lesson prices would learners consider affordable?
4.  Which skills are most in demand among the intended first users?

The first working demonstration should cover one complete transaction:
an instructor lists a skill and price, a learner finds and books the
lesson, payment is recorded, the lesson is completed, Vrya calculates
its commission and the instructor can see their earnings.

That workflow should be the team's main priority. It demonstrates the
central idea behind Vrya before the team spends time on features that
can be added later.
